## Hero — You vs. us

The two blocks under the hero buttons. The H1 and its description stay as they are (Jafar, 2026-11-17).
**Chosen by Jafar, 2026-11-17, and built.**

How it works: "You" names the contractor's wish; "Us" answers it with Uplift's system. Each worry in the "You"
sentence has its answer in the card: paying thousands → $100 a month; learning tech → done for you; losing leads →
No lead goes cold. With motion, the three worries take turns like an ad and light their answers (plan topic
`how-the-site-looks.md` § Motion).

### "You" block (on the photo)

| | Title | Text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before | Do great work. | Answer the leads we send you, then do the job. |
| **Chosen** | You're good at what you do. | You deserve a system that helps you get jobs from Google, without paying thousands, learning tech, or losing leads while you're on the job. |

"Leads we send you" made Uplift sound like a lead seller (like Angi or Thumbtack). With motion, the text shows as
"You deserve a system that helps you get jobs from Google," followed by one worry at a time: "without paying
thousands." · "without learning tech." · "without losing leads while you're on the job."

### "Us" card

| | Before | Chosen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | Everything that brings the work in | One system, done for you, for $100 a month. |
| 1 | Google profile, managed · Photos, posts and details kept fresh | **Get found on Google** · Your Google profile set up right and kept up to date |
| 2 | 5-star review funnel · More reviews, higher on Google | **More 5-star reviews** · Automatic review requests, filtered for 5 stars |
| 3 | Website with quote button & chat · Every new lead texts you | **Automated website** · Built to rank, and it turns leads into text conversations on your phone |
| 4 | Missed-call text back & follow-ups · Leads get a reply, even when you're busy | **No lead goes cold** · Missed calls get a text back, and slow replies get a follow-up |
| 5 | One-click campaigns · Referrals and repeat customers | **Repeat jobs and referrals** · One click sends your past customers an offer, so they hire you again and tell their friends |
| 6 | Business app · Leads, quotes, jobs and invoices in one place | **Your whole business in one app** · Leads, quotes, jobs, invoices and payments |
| Closing | Set up for you, then **run for you every month.** | We set it all up and **run it every month.** Setup is a one-time fee. |

The bold first words say what the contractor gets (readers mostly see the first two words of a list item, NN/g
"First 2 Words"); the small line says what Uplift does. The $100 must match the pricing section; the setup fee
there is still `$XXX`.
