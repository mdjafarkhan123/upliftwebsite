# Website redesign — research summary

Homework for the `website-redesign` campaign, done 16 November 2026. Each finding is marked **Checked** (seen on the
source), **Guess**, or **Proposal** (our recommendation, not yet agreed by Jafar). Jafar's own decisions are in
[the meeting notes](../plans/website-redesign/meeting-notes.md).

## What Uplift is

**Checked** (from `src/data/data.js` and `business.md`): a done-for-you growth system for contractors and home service
businesses. It includes a website, Google Business Profile management, a review request funnel, missed-call text back,
one-click campaigns, and Uplift's own CRM app (Android). It is sold monthly with no contract. Jafar calls it a marketing
agency.

## Competitors: structure

- **Stone Systems** (stonesystems.io), which Jafar calls the same business as his. **Checked:** the hero has a "Book A
  Call" button and team headshots, with testimonials straight after it. Five product modules each have an animated
  preview and a "See Short Demo" button. Then trades served, a 3-step process (20-minute demo call, 7–10 day build,
  25-minute launch call), six differentiators, press coverage, 15 partner logos, an FAQ, a final call to action, a
  second testimonial block, and a sticky "Book A Call" button on mobile. Price is hinted only in the FAQ ($297/month).
- **Onvert** (onvert.com/industries/contractors), which has no testimonials. **Checked:** the hero shows a phone with
  a captured lead and an automatic text reply. Then a "revenue leak" calculator, pain points, 8 systems, a 7-day install
  timeline, an FAQ, and "No credit card required • Live in 7 days • Cancel anytime". Its price is not on this page.
- **Hook Agency** (hookagency.com). **Checked:** client logos right after the hero, then process, video testimonials,
  case studies, and real starting prices on the homepage (for example, Standard Website $1,000/month).
- **Similar offers.** **Checked**, but from vendor pages only: many sell the same bundle for about $149–$750 a month,
  and most say month-to-month or no contract.
- **Price research.** **Checked:** NN/g's usability studies with business buyers (article "Show price", 2013) found
  price is the information they need most. They saw companies that hide costs as evasive, and they went to competitors
  that showed prices. Where prices vary, NN/g suggests showing sample or starting prices.

## Current copy overlap

**Checked:** several current lines closely match Stone Systems' wording. Examples: the hero title "Website design &
marketing systems for contractors…", "Simple systems that actually work. No degrees required", "5-Star Magic Review
Funnel", "Why we're 'totally unique'… just like everyone else, right?", "What working with us looks like", the 7–10 day
build step, and the four reasons (simple, affordable, no contracts, no bullsh*t). Jafar says some lines are his own or
modified (2026-11-16). **Proposal:** the new copy draft doesn't reuse Stone Systems' wording, and Jafar decides on each
of his own lines.

## Visual inspiration

- **Checked:** most "marketing agency" results on Dribbble and in the Framer marketplace are creative-studio styles:
  dark backgrounds, huge type, 3D or AI art, neon. **Guess:** that style sells branding studios to brands, not a
  growth system to tradespeople.
- **Useful references (Checked):**
  - **AdScale** (Framer template, framer.com/marketplace/templates/adscale/): light and clean, a large headline with
    one highlighted word, a phone showing stacked live notifications, two buttons ("See Case Studies" and "Book a Free
    Audit"), and a rating line under them.
  - **"RATdeck"** (Dribbble concept by OOPS Mango, an agency for plumbers): real tradesperson photos next to client
    websites shown on phones.
  - **"The systems behind sustainable growth"** (Dribbble, Artspire): a calm, soft-gradient, premium "systems" feel.
- Mobbin needs a paid plan, so it was not used.

## Proposals

**Proposal: homepage order**

1. Header with "Book a call", plus a sticky call button on mobile.
2. Hero: a phone showing Uplift's own system working (missed call followed by an automatic text, a review request, a
   new lead), with "No contract · Live in 7–10 days" under the button.
3. Client logos, hidden until real.
4. The problem: where contractors lose jobs.
5. The system: each service with a real app screenshot or short demo.
6. Sample work, labelled as samples. This replaces "Project one…six".
7. How it works: 3 steps with timings.
8. Pricing.
9. Testimonials, hidden until real.
10. Trades served, compact.
11. FAQ.
12. Final call to action.

**Proposal: concept directions for Pencil**

- **A. Product-led** (recommended): AdScale-like, light, built around the phone showing the system working.
- **B. Trade-led:** RATdeck-like, with tradesperson photos and client sites on phones.
- **C. Calm premium:** soft colours and editorial type.

**Proposal:** Design system variables use the same names as the tokens in `src/styles/critical.css`, so the build can
copy the values across directly.

**Checked:** `/app`, the legal pages and the 404 page share the header, the footer and the `critical.css` tokens, so the
new design system restyles them too.
