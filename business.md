### 📋 DETAILED INFO FOR EVERY SERVICE

(What it actually does + why contractors love it)

**CORE PACKAGE – Detailed Breakdown**

1. **High-Converting Contractor Website**  
   A brand-new, modern, fast-loading website built just for your business. Includes contact forms, service pages, before/after gallery, and easy navigation. Why it matters: Old slow websites lose leads. This one turns visitors into calls/texts instantly.

2. **Lead Capture System + AI Chatbot & Instant Quote Widget**  
   AI chatbot on your website that answers questions 24/7, qualifies leads, and gives instant price estimates. Why it matters: Leads don’t wait – they message or leave. This books jobs while you’re on another job site.

3. **Missed Call Text-Back + Call Tracking Numbers**  
   If someone calls and you miss it, they instantly get a text back. Every phone number tracks which ad or page the call came from. Why it matters: You never lose a lead again and you can see exactly where leads are coming from.

4. **Lead Notification System**  
   As soon as a lead comes in (form, chat, call, or text), you and your team get instant SMS + email alerts. Why it matters: Respond in minutes instead of hours – this alone doubles your booking rate.

5. **CRM / Job Pipeline + Basic Invoicing & Payment Links**  
   Simple dashboard that shows every lead, job status, and follow-up. Includes one-click invoicing and payment links. Why it matters: Stops leads from falling through the cracks and helps you get paid faster.

6. **Automated Follow-Ups**  
   Pre-written SMS and email sequences that automatically follow up with every lead (day 1, day 3, day 7, etc.). Why it matters: Most contractors forget to follow up. Automation turns “maybe” leads into booked jobs.

7. **Google Review Booster**  
   Automatic review request texts/emails after every job + easy review link. Why it matters: More 5-star reviews = higher ranking on Google and more trust from new customers.

8. **Quote/Estimate Request System + Online Job Scheduler**  
   Customers can request quotes or book appointments directly on your website (like a mini Calendly). Why it matters: Leads can self-book without calling – huge time saver for busy contractors.

9. **Google Business Profile Optimization + Monthly Posts**  
   We fully optimize your Google listing and post updates/photos every month. Why it matters: Most local searches start on Google – this puts you at the top of the map pack.

10. **Monthly Support, Maintenance & Performance Reporting**  
    We keep everything updated, fix issues, and send you a simple monthly report showing leads, website traffic, and results. Why it matters: You get peace of mind – everything runs smoothly without you lifting a finger.
